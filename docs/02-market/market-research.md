# Market Research

## Overview
The global market for technical interview preparation is growing rapidly, driven by:
- Increasing demand for software engineers, data scientists, and analysts
- Rising competition for tech jobs (FAANG, startups, remote roles)
- Limitations of existing static platforms (LeetCode, HackerRank)

## Market Size

### Coding Interview Prep Market
- LeetCode: 10M+ registered users
- HackerRank: 7M+ developers
- Global technical interview prep market: estimated $2B+ annually (B2C + B2B)

### Data Interview Prep Market
- DataCamp: 8M+ learners
- Kaggle: 5M+ users
- Growing at 15-20% YoY

## Growth Drivers

### 1. Tech hiring boom
Companies continue to hire technical talent despite economic fluctuations. Interviews remain highly competitive.

### 2. Static platform fatigue
Users report memorizing LeetCode solutions rather than learning problem-solving. Demand for **dynamic, non-repetitive** practice is rising.

### 3. AI adoption in education
Candidates expect AI-powered feedback and personalization – not just pass/fail.

### 4. Remote work
Global competition for roles means candidates need to stand out with superior preparation.

| Basic feedback | All platforms | No AI optimization tips |
